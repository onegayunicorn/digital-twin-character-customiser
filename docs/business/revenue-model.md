# Revenue Model

**Owner:** Business Agent · All figures are planning estimates, not verified market data.

## 1. IPS Hardware

- **Unit cost basis:** BOM per [`../hardware/sourcing-plan.md`](../hardware/sourcing-plan.md).
- **Pricing:** Sensor module $1,500-4,000 (pilot); monitoring SaaS $99-499/mo per node;
  reclamation module (phase 2) $8-20K.
- **Assumptions (Y1):** 20-40 pilot nodes; 40-60% attach SaaS.

| Metric | Y1 | Y3 |
|---|---|---|
| Units sold/leased | 30 | 500 |
| SaaS ARR | $30K | $400K |
| Hardware revenue | $90K | $800K |

## 2. VRmemories

- **Pricing:** Personal $9/mo or $89/yr; Family $19/mo; Premium session (capture +
  VR session) $199-499; B2B partnerships (custom).
- **Assumptions (Y1):** 800-2,500 subscribers; 40% annual churn; 150 premium sessions.

| Metric | Y1 | Y3 |
|---|---|---|
| Subscribers | 1,500 | 15,000 |
| ARR | $140K | $1.6M |
| Premium sessions revenue | $45K | $300K |

## 3. Theory-as-Service

- **Pricing:** Academic license free/freemium; commercial toolkit license $5-25K/yr;
  data products $1-10K/yr per dataset.
- **Grants:** SBIR/STTR + research consortium funding (target $150-400K over 18 mo).

| Metric | Y1 | Y3 |
|---|---|---|
| Licenses | 3-8 | 60 |
| License revenue | $40K | $400K |
| Grants | $150K | $500K |

## 4. Consolidated model

| Line | Y1 | Y3 |
|---|---|---|
| IPS (hw + SaaS) | $120K | $1.2M |
| VRmemories | $185K | $1.9M |
| Theory-as-Service | $190K | $900K |
| **Total** | **~$495K** | **~$4M** |

> These are plan-level estimates with explicit assumptions; refresh quarterly against
> actuals (Business Agent workflow). External investor materials must state the
> assumption basis and claims-register compliance.

## 5. Sensitivity

- IPS revenue is most sensitive to prototype cost overruns (mitigate: phase-0 validation
  before scale).
- VRmemories is most sensitive to churn and consent-flow friction (mitigate: retention
  engineering, ethics-first onboarding).
- Theory-as-Service depends on peer-review credibility (mitigate: strict protocol).
