## 1. Executive summary

The **Invisible Pressure Platform (IPP)** productizes an original physical hypothesis
(Invisible Pressure / DUP) into three complementary business lines:

1. **IPS Hardware** — invisible pressure sensing + entropy-waste recovery modules
   (nanophotonic lattice, DEC energy sphere, supercapacitor dump).
2. **VRmemories** — consent-first VR preservation of personal essence.
3. **Theory-as-Service** — licensed DUP simulation toolkit + peer-reviewed data products
   (rotation-curve pressure models, sensor data pipelines).

**Positioning:** deep-tech R&D company with honest science posture — claims register,
reproducible simulations, peer-review gates. This posture is the differentiator: in a
field crowded with AI-generated "100% complete" narratives, verifiability is the brand.

## 3. Product lines

### 3.1 IPS Hardware (flagship engineering)
- **Product:** IPS sensor modules (window-glass format), DEC energy sphere + dashboard
  (Twin-State UI), emergency supercapacitor.
- **Initial wedge:** *data-center / workstation entropy-waste monitoring* — a real,
  measurable value prop (thermal waste tracking for HPC labs) that does not depend on the
  contested energy-reclamation physics. Reclamation efficiency is a stretch goal
  (register E4: SIMULATED, 40-60% target).
- **Unit economics:** BOM-driven; see [`../hardware/sourcing-plan.md`](../hardware/sourcing-plan.md).

### 3.2 VRmemories (fastest to revenue, lowest physics risk)
- **Product:** capture app (voice/emotion/language) → memory construct → VR replay.
- **Initial wedge:** legacy & grief-care partnerships (hospice, bereavement counseling),
  family time-capsules. Ethics/consent-first is the moat.
- **Revenue:** subscription + premium capture/VR sessions + B2B partnerships.

### 3.3 Theory-as-Service (credibility engine)
- **Product:** licensed DUP simulation toolkit (monorepo packages), rotation-curve
  pressure models, IPS data pipelines; arXiv-grade peer-reviewed releases.
- **Initial wedge:** physics graduate labs & modified-gravity research groups; data
  licensing for sensor telemetry.
- **Revenue:** academic licenses, research grants, data product subscriptions.
