# Go-to-Market Strategy

**Owner:** Business Agent · **Claims envelope:** [`../theory/04-claims-register.md`](../theory/04-claims-register.md)

## 1. Brand & narrative

**Positioning statement:** "Verifiable deep tech — a pressure-field theory of the cosmos,
engineered into real sensing and human experiences, with every claim on the record."

- Public posture = **honesty by design**: claims register, published negative results,
  reproducible simulations. This is a brand asset, not a liability.
- **Forbidden messaging:** "replaces gravity", "cures diseases", "100% complete systems"
  (claims register S1-S6) — until verified.

## 3. Launch sequence (first 90 days)

1. **Week 1-2:** publish claims register + this repo (public, MIT software).
2. **Week 3-6:** submit rotation-curve simulation + honest planetary-falsification
   preprint per [`../testing/peer-review-protocol.md`](../testing/peer-review-protocol.md).
3. **Week 6-10:** VRmemories consent-first pilot with 1-2 hospice partners.
4. **Week 10-12:** IPS phase-0 bench validation kickoff (sourcing plan phase 0).

## 4. Channels & content

- **Content engine:** simulation outputs (figures, runs) — auto-published from CI to the
  public bucket (`dev-ipp-docs`).
- **Community:** modified-gravity research groups, VR grief-care forums, energy-harvesting
  engineering communities. No hype; data-first.
- **Sales motion:** founder-led for pilots; partnerships for scale.

## 5. Success metrics (90-day)

- Preprint submitted; 2+ external technical responses.
- VRmemories: 2 pilot partners signed; consent flow audited.
- IPS: phase-0 validation plan funded (grant or founder capital).
